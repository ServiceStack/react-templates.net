title: "Features"
description: "What customers and operators can do with Next License."
---

## For customers

### [Customer licenses and renewals](/docs/next-license/features/customer-licenses)

Copy or download license keys, understand update coverage, and renew or upgrade from My licenses.

### [Transfers and email preferences](/docs/next-license/features/transfers-and-preferences)

Transfer a license to another verified account and choose optional lifecycle emails.

### [Offline licenses and optional refresh](/docs/next-license/features/offline-and-refresh)

Verify licenses offline, with an optional refresh endpoint that stores no device data.

### [Releases and public downloads](/docs/next-license/features/releases-and-downloads)

Published GitHub releases become platform downloads and a Markdown changelog, with a cached fallback.

## For operators

### [Operations Center and support](/docs/next-license/features/operations-center)

Configure sales, issue and manage licenses, and review orders.

### [Promotions and tax](/docs/next-license/features/promotions-and-tax)

Let Stripe own promotion codes and tax, while each order keeps its own evidence.

### [Refunds and dispute review](/docs/next-license/features/refunds-and-disputes)

Handle refunds and disputes without promising remote revocation.

### [Lifecycle notifications](/docs/next-license/features/notifications)

Delivery links, update reminders, optional announcements and email retries.
